Research Philosophy

At the core of Bongsu Hahn’s research is a control-oriented perspective in which reliable intelligent behavior is grounded in physical understanding, dynamical modeling, estimation, data, and control methodology. His work connects rigorous system-level analysis with practical control problems by combining model-based methods and data-driven approaches according to their respective strengths. Across these efforts, he emphasizes interpretability, robustness, and experimental validation, with the broader aim of developing dependable methods for complex and changing engineering systems.
