ROCOS / RESEARCH PROJECT

Multi-DOF MEMS Microactuator with Integrated Sensing and Feedback Control

A multi-year research program integrating MEMS actuation, capacitive sensing, and precision feedback control for compact multi-axis motion.

PERIOD
2016.11 – 2021.10
ROLE
Principal Investigator
SUPPORTED BY
National Research Foundation of Korea (NRF)

I / RESEARCH CHALLENGE

The research question

How can compact actuation, sensing, and feedback stabilization be integrated across multiple motion axes?

II / RESEARCH OBJECTIVE

The research direction

Integrate rotation, two-axis tilting, vertical displacement, capacitive sensing, and precision feedback control in a multi-DOF MEMS microactuator.

III / CORE CONCEPT & SYSTEM PIPELINE

Multiple axes. Integrated feedback.

  • ROTATION Rz
  • TILT Tx
  • TILT Ty
  • VERTICAL Dz
