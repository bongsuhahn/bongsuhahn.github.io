ROCOS / RESEARCH PROJECT

Sensor Fusion for Object Detection in Dense Smoke Environments

A multimodal perception framework for detecting and localizing objects in fire-smoke environments where conventional vision becomes unreliable.

PERIOD
2021.06 – 2024.02
ROLE
Principal Investigator
SUPPORTED BY
National Research Foundation of Korea (NRF)

01 / RESEARCH CHALLENGE

The research question

How can objects be detected and localized when dense smoke makes conventional vision unreliable?

02 / RESEARCH OBJECTIVE

The research direction

Combine thermal imaging, radar, and ultrasonic sensing for 3D object detection and localization in high-temperature, low-visibility environments.

Complementary sensing. Shared geometry.
