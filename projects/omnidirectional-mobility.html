PROJECT 01 / CONTROL & ROBOTICS

AI-Driven Omnidirectional Mobility for Confined Manufacturing

An intelligent omnidirectional mobile-robot platform combining visual perception, learning-based motion planning, and adaptive state estimation for narrow and dynamic manufacturing environments.

PERIOD
2026.07 – 2027.12
ROLE
Principal Investigator
SUPPORTED BY
Ministry of SMEs and Startups (MSS) / Korea Technology and Information Promotion Agency for SMEs (TIPA)

01 / RESEARCH CHALLENGE

Agile motion where space is limited.

How can a robot move through narrow, changing manufacturing spaces while accounting for uncertainty and wheel slip?

Research objective

Integrate Vision AI, Sim-to-Real deep reinforcement learning, and adaptive sensor fusion for omnidirectional robots operating in confined manufacturing spaces.

    Confined spaces
  • 02

  • 03
