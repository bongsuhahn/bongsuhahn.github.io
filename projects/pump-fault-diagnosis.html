PROJECT 03 / MODELING & ESTIMATION

Interpretable Parameter-Space Fault Diagnosis of Centrifugal Pumps

A physics-guided diagnostic framework that transforms steady-state pump measurements into interpretable model parameters for fault analysis and classification.

PERIOD
2025.06 – 2025.11
ROLE
Principal Investigator
SUPPORTED BY
Hanil Electric · Ilseok Scholarship Foundation

01 / RESEARCH CHALLENGE

From measurements to meaningful signatures.

How can steady-state measurements reveal pump faults through physically interpretable parameters?

Research objective

Use physics-guided steady-state modeling and parameter estimation for interpretable detection and classification of centrifugal-pump faults.

  • 01
    Interpretability
  • 02
    Fault separability
  • 03
    Measurement reliability
