ROCOS / RESEARCH PROJECT

CFD-Based Design Analysis of Air–Water Separation in a Self-Priming Pump

A multiphase-flow study examining how the installation angle of an internal separation weir influences initial priming and continuous micro-bubble removal.

PERIOD
2025.05 – 2025.12
ROLE
Principal Investigator
SUPPORTED BY

01 / RESEARCH CHALLENGE

The research question

How does separation-weir geometry influence air removal during priming and continuous operation?

02 / RESEARCH OBJECTIVE

The research direction

Investigate the effect of separation-weir installation angle on initial priming and continuous micro-bubble removal.
