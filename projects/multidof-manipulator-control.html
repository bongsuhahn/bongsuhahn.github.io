ROCOS / RESEARCH PROJECT

Servo Control Architecture for a Multi-DOF Robotic Manipulator

Development of embedded servo-control hardware, joint controllers, kinematic algorithms, and integrated motion control for a multi-degree-of-freedom robotic manipulator.

PERIOD
2014.06 – 2015.07
ROLE
Principal Investigator
SUPPORTED BY

I / RESEARCH CHALLENGE

The research question

How can joint-level servo control and task-level motion planning be integrated in a multi-DOF manipulator?

II / RESEARCH OBJECTIVE

The research direction

Develop joint-level servo controllers, embedded firmware, kinematic algorithms, and integrated manipulator motion control.
