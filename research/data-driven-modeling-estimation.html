02 / RESEARCH · AREA 04

Data-Driven Modeling & Estimation

Area Overview

Data-Driven Modeling & Estimation connects measured data with mathematical models to estimate hidden states and understand system behavior.

01 / ULTIMATE GOAL

The direction of our research.

To extract meaningful structure from measured data and use it to model, estimate, monitor, and understand complex dynamical systems.

02 / CORE RESEARCH QUESTIONS

Questions that guide the work.

  1. 01

    How can hidden states or parameters be inferred from limited measurements?

  2. 02

    How can models remain informative while being computationally practical?

  3. 03

    How can data-driven methods complement physical understanding?

03 / METHODOLOGICAL APPROACH

From understanding to validation.

  1. Measured Data
  2. Model Construction
  3. State / Parameter Estimation
  4. Validation
  5. Interpretation

04 / PHILOSOPHICAL PRINCIPLE

A guiding perspective.

Data become most valuable when they are combined with structure, dynamics, and physical insight rather than treated as isolated observations.

05 / REPRESENTATIVE APPLICATIONS

Where the work applies.

  • State estimation
  • System identification
  • Fault diagnosis
  • Battery monitoring
  • Physics-guided modeling

06 / METHODS & TOOLS

How we investigate.

  • Kalman filtering
  • Nonlinear state-space modeling
  • Koopman-based modeling
  • Parameter estimation
  • Interpretable data-driven learning

Connected Areas
