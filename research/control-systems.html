02 / RESEARCH · AREA 01

Control Systems

Area Overview

Control Systems investigates how feedback and system dynamics can be used to achieve precise, stable, and reliable behavior in complex engineered systems.

01 / ULTIMATE GOAL

The direction of our research.

To develop principled control frameworks that enable engineered systems to behave reliably, stably, and efficiently under uncertainty and dynamic change.

02 / CORE RESEARCH QUESTIONS

Questions that guide the work.

  1. 01

    How can feedback be designed to guarantee stability and performance?

  2. 02

    How can dynamic behavior be shaped under disturbances and uncertainty?

  3. 03

    How can control laws remain practical for real engineering systems?

03 / METHODOLOGICAL APPROACH

From understanding to validation.

  1. Dynamic Modeling
  2. System Analysis
  3. Controller Design
  4. Stability / Performance Verification
  5. Experimental Validation

04 / PHILOSOPHICAL PRINCIPLE

A guiding perspective.

Control is not only a means of regulation, but a way of embedding structure, reliability, and intent into dynamic systems.

05 / REPRESENTATIVE APPLICATIONS

Where the work applies.

  • Motion control
  • Actuator systems
  • Feedback stabilization
  • Robust and adaptive control
  • Energy-aware control

06 / METHODS & TOOLS

How we investigate.

  • State-space methods
  • Stability analysis
  • Optimal control
  • Robust / adaptive control
  • Nonlinear control
