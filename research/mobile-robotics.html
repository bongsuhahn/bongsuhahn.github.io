02 / RESEARCH · AREA 02

Mobile Robotics

Area Overview

Mobile Robotics explores the interaction of sensing, motion, and control to enable mobile robots to operate in changing environments.

I / ULTIMATE GOAL

The direction of our research.

To build robotic systems that can move intelligently, interact safely, and perform useful tasks in real-world environments.

II / CORE RESEARCH QUESTIONS

Questions that guide the work.

  1. How should robots sense and react to changing surroundings?

  2. How can mobility remain reliable on imperfect terrain or in uncertain environments?

  3. How can perception, decision-making, and control be integrated into one system?

III / METHODOLOGICAL APPROACH

From understanding to validation.

  1. Robot Platform Design
  2. Sensing & Perception
  3. State Estimation
  4. Motion Control
  5. Field Validation

IV / PHILOSOPHICAL PRINCIPLE

A guiding perspective.

Robotic intelligence emerges not from motion alone, but from the tight integration of perception, estimation, decision-making, and action.

V / REPRESENTATIVE APPLICATIONS

Where the work applies.

  • Omnidirectional mobile robots
  • Human-aware robotic systems
  • Harsh-environment robotics
  • Agile locomotion
  • Sensor-fusion-based mobility

VI / METHODS & TOOLS

How we investigate.

  • Embedded control
  • Sensor fusion
  • Motion planning
  • Slip compensation
  • Experimental robotics
