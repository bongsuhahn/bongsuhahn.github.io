02 / RESEARCH · AREA 03

Autonomous Navigation

Area Overview

Autonomous Navigation studies how autonomous systems understand their surroundings, plan feasible paths, and navigate with confidence.

01 / ULTIMATE GOAL

The direction of our research.

To enable autonomous systems to understand where they are, decide where to go, and move safely through complex and changing environments.

02 / CORE RESEARCH QUESTIONS

Questions that guide the work.

  1. 01

    How can a robot localize itself reliably when sensing is incomplete or noisy?

  2. 02

    How can navigation remain safe in dynamic environments?

  3. 03

    How can planning and control be made robust under uncertainty?

03 / METHODOLOGICAL APPROACH

From understanding to validation.

  1. Perception
  2. Localization
  3. Mapping
  4. Planning
  5. Safe Motion Execution

04 / PHILOSOPHICAL PRINCIPLE

A guiding perspective.

Autonomy depends on more than movement; it depends on the ability to perceive structure, reason under uncertainty, and act with consistency.

05 / REPRESENTATIVE APPLICATIONS

Where the work applies.

  • GPS-free navigation
  • Vision–CAD fusion
  • Dynamic obstacle avoidance
  • Local replanning
  • Safety-aware mobility

06 / METHODS & TOOLS

How we investigate.

  • Localization and mapping
  • Path planning
  • Dynamic window methods
  • Predictive navigation
  • Multi-sensor fusion
